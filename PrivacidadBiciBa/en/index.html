Privacy Policy

How your data is handled when you use BiciBA.

Español · English

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Introduction

BiciBA is an independent app built to help people ride around Buenos Aires by bike, find bike lanes and discover routes and places worth visiting.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app may collect, how it is used and what is done to protect it.

2. Information we collect

Depending on which features you use, BiciBA may process the following data:

  • Account data: your name, your email address and a user identifier. These are the only personal data the app stores on its servers.
  • Profile picture: only if you choose to upload one. You can take it with the camera or pick it from your gallery; in both cases it is the system app that accesses them, and BiciBA only receives the photo you selected.
  • Location: precise or approximate, depending on the permission you grant. It is used while you have the app open and is not stored on any server nor shared with anyone: your phone uses it to draw your position on the map, sort routes by how close they are and guide you to the next point. You can deny or revoke the permission from your device settings; the app keeps working, sorting routes from the city centre.
  • Diagnostics and usage data: when something fails, a technical report is sent so it can be fixed, and usage statistics are recorded in aggregate form. They do not include the contents of your account.
  • Advertising identifiers: ads use the device advertising identifier and technical data, as detailed below.

BiciBA does not access your location in the background: only while the app is open and in use.

3. Data that stays only on your phone

Some things are stored only on your device and never sent to any server: the theme you chose (light or dark), the route you have in progress and the bike lane map downloaded so you can check it offline. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

4. How we use the information

The information is used to:

  • Create and manage your account, and let you log in.
  • Show your name and picture on your profile.
  • Show bike lanes and routes, place you on the map and guide you along a ride.
  • Sort routes by which one is closest to you.
  • Detect and fix app failures.
  • Show the ads that keep the project running.
  • Answer the questions you send us.

BiciBA does not sell its users' personal data.

5. Third-party services

BiciBA uses third-party services that may process information needed for it to work, including:

  • Firebase Authentication and Sign in with Google: to create your account and log you in.
  • Cloud Firestore: to store and read the app's data, including your profile.
  • Firebase Storage: to store the profile picture you upload.
  • Google Maps: to show the map and the bike lanes.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: to receive reports when the app fails so it can be fixed.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: to understand in aggregate how the app is used.
  • Firebase App Check: to verify that requests come from a legitimate copy of the app and not from a third party impersonating it.
  • Firebase Remote Config: to adjust app content, such as which routes are recommended, without releasing a new version.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. You can read Google's policies at policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Advertising — Google AdMob

BiciBA uses Google AdMob to show ads inside the app.

Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain data, such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, interactions with ads and diagnostic information. This data may be used to serve ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and maintain the advertising service.

The information collected and how it is used may vary depending on ad settings, the privacy options available and the advertising technologies involved.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policies. For more information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data when you use its partners' sites or apps

BiciBA does not directly sell its users' personal data. Data processing carried out by third-party advertising services is subject to those services' applicable policies and settings.

7. Data sharing

BiciBA does not sell or rent its users' personal information.

Data may be processed by the technology providers needed to offer the app's features. It may also be disclosed where there is a valid legal obligation or where it is necessary to protect the safety of users and of the service.

8. Data retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as needed to provide BiciBA's features, keep your account active and meet applicable legal obligations.

You can delete your account yourself from the app, under Profile → Delete account. Your profile data is erased and cannot be recovered.

You can also request it by writing to the contact address at the end of this policy.

Deletion will be carried out within a reasonable time, unless a legal obligation requires certain information to be kept for longer.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or improper disclosure.

That said, no storage or transmission system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's privacy

BiciBA is not specifically designed to collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, you can contact us to request that it be reviewed or deleted.

11. Your rights

You can request information about the personal data linked to your account, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and raise questions about how it is processed.

Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable law, including Argentine personal data protection regulations.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when BiciBA's features, the services it uses or legal requirements change. The current version will always be available at this policy's public link.
